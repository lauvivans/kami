---
title: Making cold brew at home
date: 2026-10-14
---

Cold brew steeps coarse coffee in cold water for a long time, so it comes out sweet and low in acid.

1. Grind 100 g of coffee, coarse
2. Stir it into 1 l of cold water
3. Leave it in the fridge for 12 to 18 hours, then strain

> Dilute it one to one with water or milk before serving.
